Model Accuracy Limitations

The following inherent uncertainties apply to all model outputs:

  • Gaussian plume (P-G): typical accuracy ±2–5× actual concentrations
  • Blast model (Brode / Hopkinson-Cranz): assumes free-field, unobstructed terrain
  • BLEVE fireball (Roberts 1982): assumes idealized fuel-air conditions
  • ERG 2024 (DOT/PHMSA Table 1): pre-computed conservative estimates, not site-specific
  • Radiation cloudshine (EPA FGR-12): uniform source-term assumption
  • Population estimates (US Census): uniform county density ±50–500% error possible
  • Meteorological data: sourced from third-party APIs; may not reflect on-scene conditions

🌫 Dense Gas Dispersion
Modified Pasquill-Gifford model for heavier-than-air gases. Reduces vertical spread (σ_z) by √(ρ_gas/ρ_air) to simulate ground-level pooling.

Briggs (1975) buoyant plume rise with Gaussian dispersion. Computes downwind PM2.5 and CO hazard zones from EPA AQI thresholds.
